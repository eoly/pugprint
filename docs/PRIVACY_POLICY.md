# PugPrint Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

PugPrint does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
The app works entirely offline.

- **Bluetooth**: used solely to discover and connect to your thermal printer. We do not
  use Bluetooth to determine your location.
- **Photos**: selected via the Android Photo Picker and processed **on your device only**
  to prepare them for printing. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **No** accounts, **no** analytics, **no** advertising, **no** network requests,
  **no** location collection.

Because PugPrint is intended for use by children, it is designed to comply with the
Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy by
collecting no data at all.
